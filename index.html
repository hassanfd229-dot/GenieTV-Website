About GenieTV

GenieTV is an independent Android TV media-player project. It is designed to organize and play media sources that users are authorized to access. GenieTV does not provide television channels, movies, series or streaming subscriptions.

The project uses third-party movie and television metadata services, including TMDB, to enrich content presentation. GenieTV is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Project status

GenieTV is currently under development and is not being offered for public download from this website. This page is the project's informational home.
